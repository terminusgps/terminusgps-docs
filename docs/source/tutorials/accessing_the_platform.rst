Accessing the platform
======================

The `Terminus GPS platform`_ is an always-online web application, accessible via web browser.

Alternatively, Terminus GPS also offers fully-featured native `iOS`_ and `Android`_ applications.

By the end of this tutorial, you'll be able to confidently login to the Terminus GPS platform from your desktop or mobile device.

====================
Get your credentials
====================

In order to use the `Terminus GPS platform`_, you need credentials. Your credentials are a unique username and password pair.

After your GPS tracking device was installed by a Terminus GPS professional, you were provided with credentials.

.. note:: Please reach out to `our support team`_ if you do not have credentials.

==========
Logging in
==========

After navigating to the `Terminus GPS platform`_, you'll be presented with the login form.

.. note::  For Terminus GPS mobile apps (iOS or Android), you will be presented with the login form when you open the app for the first time.

.. image:: /images/Screenshot2026-09-21143316.png

Enter your username in the ``User`` box and your password into the ``Password`` box before clicking ``Log in``.

If successful, you'll be placed at your personal Terminus GPS dashboard.

.. image:: /images/Screenshot2026-09-28-134256.png

===============
Viewing the map
===============

Now that you're logged in, you have full access to the Terminus GPS platform, including a live map showing you exactly where your GPS tracking enabled devices are located.

To view this map, select the ``Monitoring`` tab located at the top left of the dashboard screen.

.. image:: /images/monitoring-button.png

If successful, you'll be looking at all of your devices' current locations on a highly detailed map.

.. image:: /images/Screenshot2026-09-28-140702.png
